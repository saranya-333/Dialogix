Content:
The Company respects the rights and dignity of all employees. Page     : 3
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Document Title: CODE OF CONDUCT_Iagami_final
Section Title: CONFLICT OF INTEREST:
Content:
The term “conflict of interest” describes any circumstances that could cast doubt on our ability to act with total objectivity concerning the Company's interests. 3 No employee will be involved in any arrangement or circumstances, including family or other personal relationships, which might discourage him/her from acting in the best interest of the Company. No employee or his/her family member will act as a broker or on behalf of a third party in transactions involving or potentially involving the Company. Employees or their family members will not have any financial interest in any outside enterprise that is currently doing business or seeks to do business with or is a competitor of the Company if the employee comes to know that the Company is doing business with any organization/individual with which he/she is directly or indirectly involved. An employee should not be the final decision maker for any business contract or arrangement with any organization wherein his/her relatives/close friends are employed in key positions. In the event an employee finds himself/herself in a position of having to decide on such a contract or arrangement, he/she should refrain from taking such a decision and refer the matter to his/her superior for taking a decision, clearly informing his/her superior of his/her relationship with key personnel in the other organization. An employee must declare that he/she is related to any other employee of the Company as soon as he/she discovers it, through a written letter/mail to the Head

Page     : 4

Document Title: CODE OF CONDUCT_Iagami_final
Section Title: PAYMENT, GIFTS, AND ENTERTAINMENT:
Content:
The Company will not make any contribution of company funds, property, or services to a political party or religious organization, or a candidate for or holder of any Government office. No employee will offer any gifts, favors, or entertainment to others that are: i. Not consistent with customary business practice. ii. Excessive in value and/or could be construed as a bribe or pay-off in violation of applicable laws or ethical standards, such that public disclosure could 4 embarrass the Company or oneself. Employees should get the prior approval of the Business/Functional Head to incur any expenses towards entertainment. iii. Business/Functional Heads are responsible for monitoring the incurred entertainment expense patterns of their employees to ensure that the amount is used judiciously. All expenditures that do not have the necessary documentation, bills, etc. will not be reimbursed. Entertainment expenditure on alcoholic drinks will be permitted only with the specific approval of the Business/Functional Head. iv. Employees should not as a principle, accept gifts in kind or cash or enjoy any special privileges from any business associate. Complimentary gifts/Sweets may be accepted only on festive occasions if they serve the purpose of maintaining proper and good relationships with our regular business associates; however, the value of such complimentary gifts should not exceed Rs. 1000/- in value. Moreover, it is preferable that sweets, etc. if accepted on festive occasions are received only at the place of work and shared with colleagues v. Employees should not accept offers to be entertained by an individual or organizations with whom the Company transacts business. Invitations to occasional lunches/dinners hosted by our business associates may be accepted, if it serves the purpose of enhancing the relationship, after informing the immediate superior. It is essential that in the event of an employee accepting complimentary gifts (up to the value specified) or attending an occasional lunch/dinner; there should be no scope for this to be construed as having influenced the judgment of the recipient. vi. Employees will be reimbursed for outstation travel/local conveyance expenses incurred on account of official work as per policy of the Company. Employees should claim only expenses incurred and should not inflate/falsify expense claims even if these are within the prescribed limits for the individual. Under no circumstances shall personal expenses be reimbursed by the company.

Page     : 3

Document Title: LEAVE POLICY_Iagami_final (1)
Section Title: 1. CASUAL LEAVE
Content:
Objective: To attend to personal exigencies arising out of unforeseen circumstances. Eligibility: o All Employees shall be eligible for 12 days of casual leave in every calendar year. o In the case of Employees who join the Company during the calendar year, the amount of casual leave shall be prorated. o Casual leave cannot be availed for less than half a day or more than 2 days at a time. Casual leave at a stretch beyond two days shall be treated as absent without leave or Loss of pay. o Holidays and weekly off days occurring during the period of Casual leave shall be counted as part of the leave. o Casual leave cannot be combined with any other type of leave. If an Employee takes casual leave and thereafter falls ill requiring sick leave the entire period shall be converted into sick leave. o All consultants and probationers are eligible for Casual leave of 1 day for every completed month of employment, subject to a maximum of 12 days of Casual Leave per year. Accumulation: Casual leave is non-cumulative and also non-encashable. Casual leave which has not been availed shall automatically lapse at the end of every calendar year. Administration: o Employee must obtain prior sanction before proceeding on casual leave. When this is not possible, they shall as soon as practicable, inform their reporting manager of their inability to attend duty, giving reasons thereof, and should obtain approval through the prescribed application form immediately on resuming duty. o Grant of casual leave shall be subject to exigencies of business and the discretion of the leave sanctioning authority.

Job descriptions will specify whether the role is remote, hybrid, or on-site, and the selection process will accommodate remote interviews as needed. The onboarding process will include a structured program that helps new employees integrate into the company's culture and workflows, especially for remote employees. This will include virtual orientation sessions, access to an online knowledge base, and regular check-ins with HR during the first 90 days. We aim to enhance the candidate experience by ensuring timely communication throughout the recruitment process. Feedback will be provided to all candidate’s post-interview, and a dedicated point of contact will be assigned to address any queries candidates may have during the recruitment process. In recognition of the competitive nature of the IT industry, we will maintain a continuous recruitment approach for key roles, keeping an active pool of potential candidates ready for immediate hiring needs. This proactive strategy will ensure that the company can quickly respond to staffing demands.
